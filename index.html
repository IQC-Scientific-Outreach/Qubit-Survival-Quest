Your mission

Your precious qubit has been prepared in the state |0⟩. Each turn, you must place one object from your hand in its path.

Some objects measure the qubit and might destroy it. Others transform it into a new state. How long can your qubit survive?

How to play
  1. Pick one of the three objects in your hand. Click it, or enter 1, 2 or 3.
  2. Measurements (projectors and polarizers) always let the qubit through if it is in the same state and block it if it is in an orthogonal state. If the state is a superposition in the basis of the projectors, it will make it through with some probability and collapse to the basis of the measurement.
  3. Gates and rotators always let the qubit through, but can change its state.
  4. Each object the qubit survives adds one to your streak. Try to beat the odds and beat par!
Object equivalents

The below objects do exactly the same thing to the qubit depending on if we visualize it as bra-ket or photon polarization. You can switch between the two modes at any time without affecting your streak. Some objects only appear if Complex Numbers are enabled.
